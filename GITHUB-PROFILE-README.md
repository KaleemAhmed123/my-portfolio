# Kaleem Ahmed

**Backend-Heavy Full-Stack Engineer** · Noida, India

### → [kaleemahmed.in](https://kaleemahmed.in)

I build products end to end, with most of my depth in backend and systems engineering. I'm
comfortable taking an idea from an early POC through the messy middle, where requirements change,
trade-offs become real, and the system has to become more than a demo. I like starting with
something small and working, learning from it, then turning that into something that holds up in the
real world. I take ownership of the engineering underneath the product: service boundaries, data
flows, backend and infrastructure, reliability and failure modes, and whatever part of the stack the
product needs next.

What I enjoy most is what happens after something works, because that is usually where the
interesting engineering starts. How services should be split, where data should live, what should
happen asynchronously, and how the system should behave when something fails. Then the less obvious
questions: what happens when the same event arrives twice? When two requests update the same wallet
at almost exactly the same moment? When one service is down but the rest of the system should keep
moving? I've learned most of this by getting it wrong first. A race condition or an assumption that
only holds on the happy path teaches me more than a clean implementation ever does.

AI is another tool I use to solve problems, not the thing I lead with. I've built RAG and agentic
systems where the hard part was rarely the model. It was the surrounding system: giving the model
the right context, separating grounding from instructions, managing persona and memory, deciding
when retrieval is actually needed, and putting guardrails around the cases where the safest answer
is not to guess. I use AI heavily in my own workflow too, but my engineering judgement sits on top
of its output. It can help me explore an approach or write a first pass. I decide what belongs in
the system, what needs verification, and what is unnecessary complexity.
