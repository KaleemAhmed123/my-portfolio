The non-technical prep most engineers skip. Master your elevator pitch, behavioral STAR stories, negotiation tactics, and how to evaluate a company's engineering culture.

01
Introduction

Tell me about yourself

Your 60-90 second hook.

The Framework

Present: "I'm currently a Software Developer at Astrea IT, focusing on backend systems and cloud deployments."
Past (Highlight 1): "Recently, I built the DataSync ETL Hub, tying together SAP, Salesforce, and ecommerce systems into a single source of truth."
Past (Highlight 2): "Before that, I led the architecture for Eudoro, a multi-vendor marketplace with 12 microservices and an immutable payment ledger."
Future: "What excites me now is building highly resilient, data-intensive systems, which is why I'm so interested in the challenges your team is solving with [insert company specific detail]."

02
Behavioral

The STAR Method

Situation, Task, Action, Result.

Conflict / Pushback

Situation: Team wanted to deploy a large feature at once.
Action: I proposed putting it behind a feature flag and dark-launching it to 5% of traffic.
Result: Caught a massive DB lock issue in production without impacting 95% of users.

Failure

Situation: Eudoro missed some order creations during a traffic spike.
Action: I rewrote the checkout flow to decouple order creation using a Razorpay webhook and RabbitMQ.
Result: Zero dropped orders since, and introduced a DLQ for failed retries.

Leadership (Without Title)

Situation: DataSync error logs were silent on failure.
Action: I built a standardized error handler that writes failures back to Salesforce records.
Result: The support team can now see exactly why an integration failed without asking an engineer to check server logs.

05
Reverse Interview

Questions to Ask Them

Interviewing is a two-way street.
Who you are askingThe QuestionWhat it actually reveals
Engineering Manager"What is the ratio of planned work to unplanned firefighting?"Is the system on fire? Is technical debt drowning the team?
Senior Engineer"Walk me through the journey of a commit from laptop to production."Do they have CI/CD? Are deploys painful and manual, or automated and safe?
Product Manager"How are engineering estimates factored into product deadlines?"Is this a feature factory that dictates deadlines, or a collaborative environment?

Evaluation

Red Flags

When to walk away.
"We work hard and play hard." Translation: Expect 60-hour weeks and weekend deployments.
No automated testing. "We are moving too fast for tests." Translation: You will be terrified to deploy on Fridays.
The interviewer is distracted or rude. If they don't respect your time during the interview, they won't respect it when you work there.
Exploding offers. "You have 24 hours to sign." This is an intimidation tactic to prevent you from comparing offers.
